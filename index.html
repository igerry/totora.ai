Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Voice Data

We collect voice samples when you choose to create personalized stories. Your voice is used solely to generate custom storytelling experiences and is not shared with any third parties.

Story Content

Stories you create and save are stored locally on your device. We do not access or analyze your personal story content unless you explicitly choose to share it with our support team for troubleshooting purposes.

Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data to improve our app, including which features are used most frequently and crash reports. No personally identifiable information is collected.

How We Use Your Data

Personalization

Your voice data is used to create personalized storytelling experiences with custom voice cloning for your children's bedtime stories.

Service Improvement

Anonymous usage data helps us identify bugs, improve performance, and develop new features that families will love.

Customer Support

If you contact support, we may use relevant data to diagnose and resolve technical issues, with your explicit consent.

Data Security & Protection

Local Storage

All personal data including voice samples and stories are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms.

Encryption

Voice data is encrypted during transmission and storage using industry-standard encryption protocols.

No Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Parental Controls & Safety

Parental Gate

All features that could lead to purchases or external links are protected by age-appropriate parental controls.

Age-Appropriate Content

All story content is carefully curated to be age-appropriate and educational for children aged 3-12 years.

No Advertising

Totora contains no third-party advertising or behavioral tracking. Your family's privacy is our priority.

Your Privacy Rights

Access & Correction

You can access, modify, or delete your voice data and stories at any time through the app settings.

Data Export

You can export all your stories and data from the app settings at any time.

Account Deletion

You can delete all your data and voice models from the app settings. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Privacy Questions & Contact

Privacy Policy Updates

We will notify you of any significant changes to our privacy practices through the app or email if you've provided contact information.

Privacy Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@totora.app. We respond to privacy inquiries within 48 hours.

Policy Effective Date

This privacy policy is effective from December 1, 2024, and was last updated on November 6, 2024.
